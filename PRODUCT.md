# Product

## Register

product

## Users

XCRUIZT serves customers who browse, purchase, and download FiveM ReShade presets, and administrators who maintain the catalog, commerce operations, access rights, and integrations. The Admin surface is primarily used by the business owner during focused desktop workflows, with responsive access available when urgent operational work must be done from a smaller screen.

## Product Purpose

XCRUIZT is the source of truth for catalog data, prices, orders, payments, customer ownership, product versions, and download permissions. The Admin experience should let an authorized operator understand system state quickly, move between related records without losing context, and complete common edits with fewer navigation steps while preserving server-side authorization and auditability.

## Brand Personality

Premium, focused, and technically confident. The interface should feel gaming-oriented without becoming noisy, theatrical, or difficult to scan.

## Anti-references

Avoid excessive neon, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized marketing typography inside task surfaces, card-heavy dashboard templates, hidden navigation, and workflows that require repeated trips back to the Admin dashboard.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the operator in context with persistent navigation, breadcrumbs, and visible next actions.
2. Optimize common workflows for scanning and direct action, while keeping sensitive actions explicit.
3. Use one consistent interaction vocabulary across Catalog, Commerce, Customers, and System operations.
4. Preserve trustworthy system feedback with clear loading, success, error, empty, and permission-denied states.
5. Treat security, auditability, and accessibility as product behavior, not visual decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All navigation, forms, tables, drawers, and actions must work with keyboard input, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and touch targets suitable for coarse pointers.
